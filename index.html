METHOD & SAFEGUARDS

First assign each participating teacher up to the configured minimum (limited by their nominations). Then distribute remaining seats by the selected district population, using largest-remainder rounding. When districts run out of nominations, reweight seats across districts with remaining requests. Never exceed ensemble capacity or a district’s nominations.

Imported teacher-level nominations support accurate per-teacher minimums. If teacher counts or nomination totals are manually edited, the tool uses a district-level approximation (minimum × teachers, capped by nominations) and flags that distinction. Historical population figures are editable and may need updating before official use.
